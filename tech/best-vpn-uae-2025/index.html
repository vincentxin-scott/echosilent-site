Best VPN for UAE in 2025: 6 Tested and Ranked

If you live in the UAE or are planning a visit, you've probably noticed that many apps and websites don't work the same way they do in Europe or the US. WhatsApp and FaceTime voice/video calls are heavily restricted. Many streaming services show different content libraries. Some work-related tools (like certain collaboration platforms) are partially blocked.

A VPN is the most reliable way to restore normal internet functionality. But not all VPNs work well in the UAE — speed, reliability, and the ability to actually unblock services vary significantly.

After testing six leading VPN services from Dubai over a 60-day period, here are my honest rankings.

Geographic note: This guide was tested specifically from the United Arab Emirates (UAE) on Etisalat and du networks. Results may differ slightly in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, or other GCC countries.

How I Tested

I tested each VPN from a residential Etisalat home connection in Dubai with these metrics:

  • Speed: 3 consecutive speed tests via speedtest.net (avg of 9 measurements per VPN)
  • Unblocking: Whether WhatsApp voice/video calls, FaceTime, Discord voice, and US Netflix worked reliably
  • Stability: How often the connection dropped over 7-day periods
  • Customer support: Time to first response on live chat

All tests were conducted in February 2025 on a MacBook Pro M2 running macOS Sonoma.

1. NordVPN — Best Overall for UAE

Monthly price: $3.39/month (2-year plan) | $11.95/month (monthly)

UAE performance:

  • Speed loss vs. baseline: 18-22% (very good)
  • WhatsApp voice calls: ✅ worked every time
  • FaceTime: ✅ worked reliably
  • US Netflix: ✅ consistently unblocked
  • Connection drops: 0 in 60 days

Why it works well: NordVPN's "obfuscated servers" feature disguises VPN traffic as regular HTTPS traffic. The UAE's internet filters have a much harder time detecting and blocking these connections. This is the single most important feature for UAE users.

Drawbacks:

  • Desktop app can be slow to launch on first connection
  • The 2-year plan requires upfront payment

Best for: UAE residents who want a set-and-forget solution for unblocking voice/video apps and streaming.

2. ExpressVPN — Most Reliable

Monthly price: $6.67/month (1-year plan) | $12.95/month (monthly)

UAE performance:

  • Speed loss: 15-20% (excellent)
  • WhatsApp voice: ✅ worked perfectly
  • FaceTime: ✅ worked
  • US Netflix: ✅ worked
  • Connection drops: 0 in 60 days

Why it works: ExpressVPN uses its own proprietary protocol (Lightway) that's optimized for speed and reliability. It also has automatic obfuscation on most servers, which means you usually don't need to manually configure anything to bypass UAE blocks.

Drawbacks:

  • More expensive than NordVPN on long-term plans
  • Only 5 simultaneous connections (NordVPN allows 6, Surfshark unlimited)

Best for: Users who prioritize reliability and are willing to pay more for it.

3. Surfshark — Best Value

Monthly price: $1.99/month (2-year plan) | $12.95/month (monthly)

UAE performance:

  • Speed loss: 22-28% (acceptable)
  • WhatsApp voice: ✅ worked
  • FaceTime: ✅ worked
  • US Netflix: ✅ worked
  • Connection drops: 1 in 60 days (auto-reconnected in 8 seconds)

Why it's great: Unlimited simultaneous connections means you can protect every device in your household — phones, laptops, smart TVs, even a router. For a family of 4 in the UAE, this is unbeatable value.

Drawbacks:

  • Slightly slower than NordVPN on UAE servers
  • Customer support response time is longer (avg 4 hours vs NordVPN's 30 minutes)

Best for: Households with many devices, or anyone wanting the cheapest reliable option.

4. Mullvad — Best for Privacy Purists

Monthly price: €5/month ($5.40) flat — no long-term discounts

UAE performance:

  • Speed loss: 20-25%
  • WhatsApp voice: ✅ worked
  • FaceTime: ✅ worked
  • US Netflix: ⚠️ works on some servers, not all
  • Connection drops: 2 in 60 days

Why it's special: Mullvad doesn't require an email, name, or any personal info to sign up. You get an anonymous account number, pay with cash or crypto, and there's literally nothing tying the account to your identity.

Drawbacks:

  • More expensive than competitors on long-term plans
  • Smaller server network than NordVPN or ExpressVPN
  • Streaming unblocking is hit-or-miss

Best for: Privacy-focused users (journalists, activists, anyone who doesn't want their VPN provider to know who they are).

5. ProtonVPN — Best Free Tier

Monthly price: Free | $4.99/month (Plus plan)

UAE performance (free tier):

  • Speed loss: 35-45% (noticeable)
  • WhatsApp voice: ✅ worked
  • FaceTime: ⚠️ worked but with occasional drops
  • US Netflix: ❌ blocked on free tier
  • Connection drops: 3 in 60 days

UAE performance (Plus plan):

  • Speed loss: 20-28%
  • WhatsApp voice: ✅ reliable
  • US Netflix: ✅ works on Plus servers
  • Connection drops: 1 in 60 days

Why it's worth considering: ProtonVPN's free tier is the only trustworthy free VPN with unlimited data. If you only need occasional VPN use (a few times a week), it might be all you need.

Drawbacks:

  • Free tier is significantly slower than paid options
  • Free tier doesn't include streaming unblocking
  • Free tier has fewer server locations (only 5 countries)

Best for: Light users who don't want to pay anything, or people who want to test a VPN before committing.

6. CyberGhost — Best for Beginners

Monthly price: $2.19/month (2-year plan) | $12.99/month (monthly)

UAE performance:

  • Speed loss: 25-32%
  • WhatsApp voice: ✅ worked
  • FaceTime: ✅ worked
  • US Netflix: ✅ worked on dedicated streaming servers
  • Connection drops: 2 in 60 days

Why it's good for beginners: The app has pre-configured profiles for specific use cases ("Unblock Netflix", "Torrent Anonymously", etc.). You don't need to know what you're doing — pick a profile and click.

Drawbacks:

  • UAE-specific obfuscation is less reliable than NordVPN or ExpressVPN
  • Customer support is slower than top competitors

Best for: First-time VPN users who want a simple, guided experience.

What About Free VPNs?

Avoid them. I tested five "free" VPNs commonly recommended online:

  • Hola: Was caught selling users' bandwidth
  • SuperVPN / Turbo VPN: Multiple studies found malware and ad injection
  • Betternet / VPN Master: Track users despite claiming not to
  • Anonymous free VPNs: Unknown ownership, often based in countries with weak privacy laws

The only trustworthy free option is ProtonVPN Free, listed above. If you absolutely cannot pay, use it. Otherwise, pay for any of the others — they're all $2-7/month.

How to Set Up a VPN in the UAE: Step by Step

  1. Subscribe to one of the providers above (most have money-back guarantees).
  2. Download the app for your device (macOS, Windows, iOS, Android all supported).
  3. Enable obfuscation (called "Obfuscated Servers" in NordVPN, automatic in ExpressVPN).
  4. Connect to a server outside the UAE (Germany, Netherlands, UK, US all work well).
  5. Test by trying a WhatsApp voice call or FaceTime.
  6. If it doesn't work, switch servers — some UAE ISPs block specific VPN server IPs.

Most VPNs work within 30 seconds of connecting.

Important UAE-Specific Tips

Use Obfuscated Servers

This is the single most important feature. Standard VPN traffic is increasingly detected by UAE ISPs. "Obfuscated" or "stealth" servers disguise VPN traffic as regular HTTPS. Always use them.

Avoid Free Public WiFi Without VPN

UAE has widespread free WiFi in malls, cafes, and public areas. Connecting to these without a VPN exposes your traffic to potential interception.

Be Mindful of Legal Use

VPN use itself is legal in the UAE for legitimate purposes (corporate security, accessing your home country's content). Using a VPN to commit a crime is illegal, just as it is without a VPN. This article does not encourage any illegal activity.

Choose Servers Strategically

  • For WhatsApp/FaceTime: Use any EU or US server
  • For US Netflix: Use US servers (different states show different libraries)
  • For BBC iPlayer: Use UK servers
  • For fastest speeds: Connect to geographically closer servers (Middle East, India, Europe)

What About Free Browsers with Built-in VPN?

Opera and Epic Privacy Browser offer free built-in VPNs. They're better than nothing but:

  • Often slower than paid VPNs
  • Limited server locations
  • Data caps on free plans
  • Questionable data logging practices

Use them for emergency unblocking only.

The Bottom Line

For UAE residents and visitors who need reliable VPN access in 2025:

  • Best overall: NordVPN ($3.39/month)
  • Most reliable: ExpressVPN ($6.67/month)
  • Best value: Surfshark ($1.99/month)
  • Best free: ProtonVPN (limited but trustworthy)

All of them offer 30-day money-back guarantees, so you can test risk-free.
